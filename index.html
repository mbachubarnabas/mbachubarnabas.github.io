03 — CASE STUDIES

Practical support scenarios.

These projects demonstrate how I think through common customer and IT support situations.

01
ZendeskTicketingDocumentation

Customer Support Ticket Workflow

Designed a structured workflow for receiving, documenting, prioritising and following up on customer issues.

Scenario

A customer reports a service problem and expects a quick, clear response.

My approach

Verify customer details, capture the issue accurately, categorise it, set priority, add internal notes and document the next action.

Outcome

A repeatable workflow that keeps the customer informed and gives the support team a clear record.

02
FreshdeskPaymentsEmpathy

Dispense Error Support Case

Built a realistic payment/ATM dispense-error scenario and customer response.

Problem

A customer reports that their account was debited but cash was not received.

Response approach

Acknowledge the frustration, verify transaction details, explain the investigation process, provide a ticket/reference number and set a clear expectation for follow-up.

Key skill: Empathetic communication + structured escalation
03
IT SupportTroubleshootingEscalation

Login & Password Recovery

Created a support flow for a user who completed a password reset but still could not access an account.

Investigation

Confirm the account, check whether the reset completed, verify credentials are being entered correctly and rule out common browser/device issues.

Escalation

If the issue persists, document the troubleshooting already performed and escalate with the relevant account and error details.

Key skill: Logical troubleshooting + clean documentation
04
HubSpotCRMData Quality

Customer Contact Management

Practised maintaining accurate customer information and using CRM records to support better service.

Scenario

A customer contacts support with an existing account but some contact information is outdated.

Approach

Verify the customer's details, update the relevant CRM fields, record the interaction and ensure the next support agent can understand the history.

Key skill: CRM hygiene + customer history management
